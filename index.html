Company Culture Dossiers

The Traditional Corp.

They only look at past performance and skills. They hire people who are great on paper. They do not test for character.

The Party Startup

They sell a fake reality in their want ads. They say "it is a party here every day". Employees quickly discover it feels like a dungeon.

The Honest Company

They are brutally honest in interviews. They admit there is stress and dark days, but the camaraderie is incredibly high.
